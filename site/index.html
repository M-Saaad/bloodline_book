A goat's page in the app.

One page per goat

Everything you know about her, where you can find it.

Look a goat up by name or tag number. Her page holds what would otherwise be spread across a notebook, a spreadsheet and your memory.

  • Tag, tattoo and breed
  • Date of birth
  • Dam and sire
  • Breedings and kiddings
  • Weights over time
  • Treatments and withdrawal days
  • Which pasture she's in
  • Farm income and expenses

In the barn

Four questions it answers on the spot.

Who's due?

Record a breeding and the app works out her due window. Does that are close show on the Today screen and on the breeding calendar.

Whose kid is this?

Log a kidding and each kid is saved with its dam and sire already linked, so you never have to dig for it later.

What did she get, and when?

Every treatment is saved on the goat's page with its date. Meat and milk withdrawal days show as a reminder.

Withdrawal days are reminders, not veterinary advice. Follow the label and your vet.

Is he growing?

Weigh one goat, or run a weigh session for the whole pen. Each goat keeps its own weight history.

The blood tree

Pedigrees that build themselves.
