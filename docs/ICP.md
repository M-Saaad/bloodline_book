# Ideal Customer Profile — Bloodline Book

## Who this is for

US (and UK/AU) **ADGA-registered dairy goat breeders** running **25–100 goats**, typically Nigerian Dwarf, Nubian, LaMancha, Alpine, or Saanen herds.

## What they need

- Structured health records (vaccines by **disease target**, dewormers by **drug class**, FAMACHA/BCS/FEC)
- Breeding and kidding tracking with reminders
- Milk/lactation history (Phase 2)
- A system that turns records into **actionable reminders**, not just storage

## What they do not want

- Partner-equity / boarding (Palai) workflows from the original single-farm app
- Pakistan-specific breeds, PKR-only finance, or real farm demo data
- AI diagnosis or dose recommendations — the app records what was done; vets decide treatment

## Go-to-market (current phase)

Single-tenant, **concierge provisioning** — no self-serve signup until there is real demand signal.
